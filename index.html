Everything your medicine cabinet should have been doing all along.

02 · Search & prescriptions

Find a medicine by name, or let the prescription do the typing.

Search your stock in a second. Or snap the doctor's prescription: MediLookup pulls out every medicine on it and tells you what you already have at home, so you only buy what's actually missing.

03 · Same-salt alternatives

Brand unavailable? Get the same salt from another brand.

When the exact brand isn't at home or at the chemist, MediLookup suggests medicines with the same salt and strength from other brands, so you can ask your doctor or pharmacist about a swap with confidence.

04 · Order nearby

Really missing something? Order it from the store down the road.

When a medicine isn't in your home stock, MediLookup shows the nearest medical retailers that have it. Place the order in the app and it's added to your stock once it arrives.

05 · Schedules & reminders

One app for the whole family's pills.

Set a schedule for each family member, from Dad's morning tablet to your child's night-time syrup. MediLookup sends a notification right on time and keeps track of every dose taken or missed.
